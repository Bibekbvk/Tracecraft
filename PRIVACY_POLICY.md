# Privacy Policy for TraceCraft

**Last updated: September 02, 2026**

Welcome to **TraceCraft** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy describes how TraceCraft collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our mobile application (**"TraceCraft - AR Drawing & Tracing"**).

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account Information**: When you register an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials. We use this to verify your identity via a 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) and maintain your user profile.
* **User-Generated Content**: If you choose to publish artwork to the Community Gallery, your sketch image, title, and public display name are stored to make them visible to other users.
* **Feedback & Support**: Any information or email address you provide when submitting feedback or contacting support.

### B. Permissions & On-Device Data
* **Camera Access**: TraceCraft uses your device camera solely in real-time to provide the **Camera Lucida AR optical tracing overlay**. We do **NOT** record, store, or transmit your live camera video feed to external servers. Tracing occurs 100% locally on your device hardware.
* **Photo Library / Storage Access**: Used only when you choose to import reference photos from your gallery for sketching or save your completed drawings.
* **Local Storage (Hive & Cache)**: App preferences, drawing sessions, streaks, and offline settings are stored locally on your device.

### C. Automatically Collected Information & Third-Party Services
We partner with third-party service providers that may collect information used to identify your device:
* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: We display advertisements via AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and general diagnostics to serve personalized or contextual ads according to Google's privacy terms.
  * [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  * [Google Advertising Technologies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
* **Google Play Services**: For analytics, in-app purchases (if applicable), and app integrity checks.
  * [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Firebase Services**: Used for secure authentication, database persistence, and crash diagnostics.
  * [Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:
1. Provide, maintain, and enhance the AR Camera Lucida tracing features.
2. Authenticate user accounts and protect against unauthorized access or brute-force attempts.
3. Enable sharing and rating of artwork in the Community Gallery.
4. Deliver non-intrusive advertisements and grant 24-hour ad-free session rewards.
5. Provide technical support and resolve bugs.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Security

* **Data Security**: We implement industry-standard security safeguards, including HTTPS transport security, cryptographic session tokens, sliding-window rate limiting, and encrypted storage to protect your data against unauthorized access.
* **Data Retention**: Account information is retained as long as your account remains active. Local drawings and settings stay on your device and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage.

---

## 4. Guest Mode & Anonymity

TraceCraft offers a **Guest Artist Mode**. In Guest Mode, you can use all AR tracing, filtering, grid, and camera tools without providing any email or personal information.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

TraceCraft does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to:
* Access the personal information we hold about you.
* Request deletion of your account and associated Community Gallery posts.
* Opt-out of personalized advertising through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID**).

To request data deletion, contact us at the email provided below.
