# Privacy Policy for TraceCraft

**Last Updated**: September 1, 2026

TraceCraft ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our optical Camera Lucida drawing assistant application.

---

## 1. Information We Access and Process

### A. Camera Feed (On-Device Real-Time Processing)
- **Purpose**: TraceCraft accesses your device camera to provide a live preview of your drawing paper underneath the reference overlay.
- **Strict Privacy Guarantee**: The live camera feed is processed **strictly in real-time on your local device**. We **never** record, store, transmit, or sell your live camera feed or private surroundings to our servers or any third parties.

### B. Photos & Storage Access
- **Local Tracing**: Selecting photos from your device library for tracing happens purely within your device storage.
- **Community Showcase Uploads**: When you explicitly choose to publish your finished drawing to the Community Showcase, your selected drawing image, title, and caption are uploaded to **Firebase Cloud Storage** and **Cloud Firestore**.

### C. Device & Anonymous Identifiers
- **Firebase Authentication**: We generate an anonymous cryptographic user ID on your device to attribute your artwork uploads, likes, and 1–5 star ratings without requiring personal registration or passwords.
- **Hive Local Database**: Tracing canvas transformation matrices (zoom, pan, rotation), streak counts, and starting opacity preferences are stored locally on your device via encrypted Hive boxes.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

### A. Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)
- Used to deliver banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded ads.
- May collect: Google Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA), coarse location, and ad interaction analytics to serve non-personalized or personalized ads.
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### B. Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Anonymous Auth)
- Used to power the Community Gallery showcase and user feedback submissions.
- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

### C. Pexels API
- Used to provide royalty-free reference sketches and search photos. Search queries are transmitted anonymously to the Pexels API.
- [Pexels Privacy Policy](https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/)
